I had a starting accessibility score of 96. The audit found issue with the contrast between the color of my links and the background of the webpage. Too fix this I committed multiples changes to the text color of my navigation bar, making the links a darker color. I also committed the same changes to the links within the main body of my webpage. Lastly, the contrast color of the "send" button in my contact section was too light so I made the send button the same color as my links. My final accessibility color is 100.
